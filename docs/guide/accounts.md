# Accounts

You do not need an account to search TLCMap, browse public layers, download results or
use the Web Services API. You need one to contribute data, save searches, work with
texts, or collaborate with other people on a layer.

Accounts are free.

## Creating an account

Choose **Register** in the top right of any page. You will be asked for:

- **Name** — how you appear to collaborators and as the contributor of a public layer.
- **Email address** — must not already be registered.
- **Password**, entered twice.

You also have to accept the TLCMap Conditions of Use before the account is created.

### Password rules

The password must:

- be between 8 and 16 characters
- contain at least one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter, one digit and one
  character that is none of those
- not repeat any single character five or more times in a row
- not contain any part of your name or email address

That last rule is easy to trip over. If your email address is `jsmith@example.edu.au`,
then `example`, `jsmith`, `edu` and `au` cannot appear anywhere in the password.

## Verifying your email address

Depending on how the site is configured, you may be sent a verification email when you
register. Until you follow the link in it, you can log in but cannot reach the pages that
create or edit data. You can ask for the email to be sent again from the page that
prompts you.

## Logging in and resetting your password

Choose **Log in** in the top right. If you have forgotten your password, use the
forgotten-password link on the login form: TLCMap emails you a reset link, and the new
password has to satisfy the same rules as above.

## Your profile

**Your name → My profile** opens the profile page, which has three separate forms.

| Form | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| General | Your display name. |
| Password | Your password. Requires your current password. |
| Email | Your email address, entered twice. Requires your password. |

Changing your email address sends a notification to both the old and the new address.
Changing your password sends a confirmation email.

## Finding your work

Once you are logged in, the **My Maps** menu holds everything you have created:

| Menu item | Page |
| --- | --- |
| My layers | `myprofile/mydatasets` — layers you own or collaborate on |
| My multilayers | `myprofile/mycollections` — your multilayers |
| My texts | `myprofile/mytexts` — documents you have uploaded for geoparsing |
| My searches | `myprofile/mysearches` — your saved searches |

## Roles on a layer

Access to a layer is per-person, not per-account-type. When someone shares a layer with
you, they choose one of four roles:

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including deleting the layer. Set when the layer is created. |
| **Admin** | Edit the layer and its records, and manage other people's access. |
| **Collaborator** | Add and edit records, including bulk uploads. Cannot manage access. |
| **Viewer** | See the layer and its records. Cannot change anything. |

See [Sharing your work](./sharing) for how to grant these.
